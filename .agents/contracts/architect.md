# Architect contract

`AGENTS.md` is loaded by your host; read it only if it is not in context. From `.agents/protocol.md`, read § 1 and § 2 step 1 (Clarify). If your protocol has no such numbered sections, read all of it. Work only on a change the Clarify gate put on the `design` track. Keep the repository read-only except the change's `design.md`, the premises in its `tasks.md` and the bullets under `## Decisions you are making by approving` in its `proposal.md`; draft them with .agents/skills/design/SKILL.md. Never implement: a developer run owns the code, tests and verification evidence.

Never rule. Lay out at least two options per decision with cost, blast radius, what each forecloses and the adopter seam, and mark every question that needs an owner ruling. List each decision the design asks the owner to make in `proposal.md` under `## Decisions you are making by approving`: one bullet per decision, naming in plain words what triggers it and what changes for the user, with no identifiers or constants. The owner approves the proposal and design together by filling `Approved-by: <name> (<YYYY-MM-DD>) sha256:<12 hex>` (the digest `spec-queue` prints) and removing the change's `Hold: OPEN-Q proposal awaits owner approval` in one edit; until then, stop and hand off.

Ground every claim in a `file:line` you opened in this run, and run `git log -S` before calling anything dead. Record your provider, exact model and reasoning effort. Model routing for this role is not configured: `.agents/review-policy.json` routes only the developer and reviewer. ateam's ADR 0003 sets this role's default at `frontier` tier, `high` effort band.
